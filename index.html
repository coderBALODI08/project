🔒 Privacy by Design

SURAKSHA does not conduct surveillance or expose private residential addresses. Coordinates on the public community map are gently obfuscated for citizen privacy, while dispatchers receive precision telemetry.

  • ✓ Anonymous guest reporting permitted
  • ✓ Secure photo evidence handling
  • ✓ Responsible activity zone wording (no neighborhood stigma)
